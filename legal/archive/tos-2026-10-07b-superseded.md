### 4. Student data and privacy

Company processes personal information of Users, including student education records, solely to provide, maintain, secure, and support the Application for the School. The School retains ownership of all student data.

**FERPA.** When Willow serves a school, Willow acts as a school official under FERPA. It uses education records only for the school's purposes and does not redisclose them without the school's written permission, except as FERPA allows. Parents and eligible students exercise their rights to inspect, amend, and consent through their school.

**AI use.** Willow's AI features, including the Alma assistant, send their prompts to Anthropic, the only generative AI provider Willow uses. A prompt contains the student's first name, grade, a short summary of the student's goals relevant to the conversation, and the student's typed message. Prompts never include email, phone, address, date of birth, demographic fields, or other students' data. Alma conversations are kept for 24 months after their last activity, then deleted.

**OpenAI.** OpenAI is used only to turn reference content (career and school descriptions) and search queries into numerical search data called embeddings. It generates no content and receives no student identifiers.

**Training.** Willow does not sell student data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models. Willow's AI providers operate under enterprise terms that prohibit training their models on Willow's data.

**Safety.** Every AI interaction is screened automatically, and content that raises a safety concern is routed to the School's designated staff for review. The AI assistant provides information and support only; it does not make or determine decisions affecting a student's academic or postsecondary record.

**Data sharing.** Willow shares student data only with the service providers listed below and in these school-directed ways: (a) Common App: once a school turns on its Common App integration, and only after a student links their Common App account and signs Common App's FERPA waiver, counselors, school administrators and the student's assigned teachers submit school reports, recommendations and related forms, which can include GPA, class rank, transcripts and letters, to the colleges on that student's Common App list; (b) counselors and school administrators can email a student's documents to a college's admissions address on file. Willow's only message to a parent or guardian is a request to sign an Early Decision agreement, sent only when the student asks.
